## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored for Cindyrella specifically.** Numbered section labels rendered by one shared `SectionTitle` across every homepage section, a dark `Philosophy` band that exists purely to ground the palette, a skin-type quiz modal bespoke to a considered skincare purchase, PH-specific commerce conventions (₱, GCash/Maya, Shopee/Lazada-style "To Pay / To Ship / To Receive" order tabs), and dry, non-templated copy voice throughout. The three-color discipline (gold scoped to ratings only) holds almost everywhere.

**Deterministic scan**: the bundled anti-slop detector returned clean (`[]`) on all 13 customer-facing targets — its rule set targets animation/gradient/typography "AI slop" patterns, which this codebase doesn't exhibit. That clean result should be read narrowly: it does not cover the accessibility gaps found manually below, which the detector isn't designed to catch.

**Manual/code evidence layer** (the real second signal here, since no browser was available): sitewide, customer-facing `htmlFor` label association is essentially absent (0 occurrences across `app/**`, effectively 0 in `components/**` in scope) — every label is a visual sibling of its input, not programmatically linked. Two wishlist-toggle controls (`components/ui/Card.tsx`, `components/sections/BestSellers.tsx`) are `<span role="button">` with no `tabIndex`/`onKeyDown`, so they're not keyboard-operable at all — despite the correct pattern existing elsewhere in the same codebase (`account/purchases/page.tsx` implements the identical control correctly). Several primary touch targets render under the ~44px guideline (Navbar icon buttons at 36px, wishlist toggle at 32px, PDP qty stepper ~32px) while the equivalent cart-page control correctly uses 44px — inconsistency, not a universal gap.

## Overall Impression

The brand execution is genuinely strong and specific — this doesn't read like a template. The single biggest opportunity is that the site's *mechanics* haven't caught up to its *voice*: a beautifully-voiced site loses the user's cart on refresh, drops their intended purchase after a forced login, and ends the entire funnel at a dead checkout button. None of that is a visual problem; all of it is a trust problem.

## What's Working

1. **Skeleton-matches-final-layout discipline.** Nearly every route ships a bespoke skeleton whose grid/dimensions/row-counts are hand-matched to real content, preventing layout shift — well beyond typical demo-app polish.
2. **Consistent, warm empty-state pattern.** Cart/wishlist/orders/shop-search all share one icon-circle + reassuring, context-specific copy + single CTA shape.
3. **Disciplined three-color system.** Gold stays scoped to ratings/reviews across ~15 files reviewed, with only one borderline decorative use — unusually consistent brand rigor.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Checkout is a dead end for the entire purchase flow**
- **Why it matters**: "Proceed to Payment" (`app/cart/page.tsx:172-177` desktop, `:195-200` mobile) only shows a toast that checkout "isn't wired up yet." This is the terminal step of the flow under review — no persona can complete a purchase.
- **Fix**: Style/label the button so its unfinished state is legible before the click (e.g. visibly disabled or "Coming Soon") rather than presenting an identical primary CTA to a working one.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Add-to-cart/wishlist login gate loses the user's original action**
- **Why it matters**: `components/ui/Card.tsx:13-19`, `components/sections/BestSellers.tsx:53-60`, `library/store.tsx:87-93` all open `LoginModal` on an unauthenticated attempt and abort — after `signIn()` succeeds, nothing replays the original add-to-cart/wishlist call. The user must relocate the product and re-click, unsure if the first click "did anything."
- **Fix**: Capture the pending `{productId, qty, sizeId}` before opening the modal and replay it from `signIn()`'s success path.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] No state persistence — refresh silently wipes cart, wishlist, and login**
- **Why it matters**: `library/store.tsx`'s `StoreProvider` holds cart/wishlist/login in plain `useState` with no localStorage/cookie backing. Any refresh, back-navigation, or new tab wipes the session with zero warning or restore prompt.
- **Fix**: Persist cart/wishlist/session to `localStorage` and rehydrate alongside the existing `useMounted()` hydration-safety pattern already used elsewhere.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Sitewide form/control accessibility gaps (unlabeled inputs, non-keyboard-operable controls, sub-44px targets)**
- **Why it matters**: Every customer-facing form field (Contact, Login/Signup, account profile, password change) has a visually-adjacent but not `htmlFor`-linked label — a real WCAG name/role/value gap. Two wishlist-toggle controls are keyboard-unreachable entirely (`Card.tsx`, `BestSellers.tsx`), while the identical control is done correctly elsewhere in the same codebase. Primary touch targets (Navbar icons, wishlist toggle, PDP qty stepper) render smaller than the cart page's own correct 44px equivalent.
- **Fix**: Wire `htmlFor`/`id` pairs across the flagged forms; convert the two `role="button"` spans to real `<button>` elements (or add `tabIndex={0}` + `onKeyDown` matching the pattern already correct in `account/purchases/page.tsx`); bring the undersized controls up to the 44px standard the cart page already sets.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit`

**[P2] Homepage catalogue filters are fully-styled but dead**
- **Why it matters**: `components/sections/Catalogue.tsx:27-49` renders Category/Price/Rating selects marked `// TODO`, visually indistinguishable from the working filters on `/shop`. A visitor's first filtering attempt happens here, before `/shop` — getting no response reads as broken, not unbuilt.
- **Fix**: Wire to the same filter state `/shop/page.tsx` already implements, or disable/remove until they do something.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (confused first-timer)**: Interrupted by `WelcomePromoModal` at a flat 1.4s timer before finishing the Hero headline. Tries the dead homepage filters and gets no feedback. Clicks "Add to Bag," gets bounced into login with no warning, and after signing up the product isn't in the bag (no resumption — see P1). Reaches checkout and hits the dead-end toast, which reads as a broken site rather than a preview limitation.

**Casey (distracted mobile user)**: The mobile fixed Total/Proceed bar on `/cart` is a genuinely good thumb-zone pattern. But PDP qty-stepper and size-selector buttons sit close together with no explicit min-height toward 44px — easy one-handed mis-tap. None of the three motion primitives (`FadeIn`/`RevealIn`/`TypeReveal`) check `prefers-reduced-motion`, so a 12-section scroll-animated homepage adds unconditional perceived latency on a slow connection.

**Riley (stress-tester)**: Refreshing at any point silently wipes cart/wishlist/login (biggest stress-test failure found). Unauthenticated `/cart` and `/wishlist` correctly show a login-required modal rather than crashing. Unknown product/journal/ritual IDs all render clean fallbacks with working back-links — good defensive handling. The per-item cap is correctly enforced across size variants of the same product, not just per-variant.

## Minor Observations

- Two divergent Privacy Policy implementations (`app/account/privacy/page.tsx` hardcodes its own text; `app/pages/privacy-policy/page.tsx` is CMS-driven) — a real trust/legal risk since they can drift, but scoped as minor relative to the P0-P1s above.
- `WelcomePromoModal` fires on a flat timer regardless of engagement — works against the "editorial, considered" positioning.
- `account/notifications/page.tsx` toggles give no save confirmation, unlike every other settings action in the app.
- Newsletter's decorative star row uses `text-gold` outside a literal rating context — a mild stretch of the "gold = ratings only" rule.

## Questions to Consider

- What if "Add to Bag" replayed automatically after login, instead of just returning the user to where they were?
- Does the checkout button need to look identical to a working CTA while it's still a placeholder?
- Is the homepage the right place for live filters at all, or should it link out to `/shop` with the filter pre-set instead of duplicating dead controls?
